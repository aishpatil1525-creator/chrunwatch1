ChurnWatch Internship Analytics Report

Executive summary, methodology, mathematical definitions, and model evaluation.

1. Problem Statement & Objectives

In B2B SaaS business models, customer acquisition costs typically exceed retention costs by 5x to 7x. ChurnWatch addresses revenue loss by consolidating multi-dimensional telemetries (subscription details, usage analytics, support escalations) to compute early risk signals before renewal dates arrive.

  • Objective 1: Identify leading indicators of customer attrition with >85% classification accuracy.
  • Objective 2: Segment 1,250 accounts into actionable risk tiers to prioritize retention outreach.
  • Objective 3: Provide Customer Success Managers (CSMs) with contextual mitigation playbooks.

2. KPI Mathematical Definitions

Monthly Customer Churn Rate:
Churn Rate (%) = (Accounts Churned in Period / Total Accounts at Start of Period) × 100
Net Renewal Rate (NRR):
Renewal Rate (%) = ((Total Accounts at Renewal - Churned Accounts) / Total Accounts at Renewal) × 100
Multi-Factor Churn Risk Index (0-100):
Score = 0.35 × (Engagement Delta Risk) + 0.25 × (Seat Underutilization) + 0.25 × (Support Friction) + 0.15 × (Contract Vulnerability)
